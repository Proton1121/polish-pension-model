# Polish pension system

The vocabulary of the Polish old-age pension system (ZUS, with PPK later) as this project models it. Each term is English, then gives the Polish legal term the cited acts use and the article that defines it.

## Language

### People and records

**Insured person**:
A person covered by the ZUS old-age pension insurance, whose pension is being calculated. Polish: *ubezpieczony* (art. 24 FUS).
_Avoid_: member, participant (reserved for PPK's *uczestnik*)

**Account**:
ZUS's record of a person's valorized pension contributions, excluding those recorded on the sub-account. In law the sub-account sits inside the account, but the two are valorized differently and always kept apart here. Polish: *konto ubezpieczonego* (art. 40 SUS; art. 25 FUS).
_Avoid_: NDC account, individual account, ZUS account

**Sub-account**:
ZUS's separate record of the share of pension contributions (and assets transferred from OFE) that is valorized by nominal GDP growth. Polish: *subkonto* (art. 40a SUS).
_Avoid_: OFE account, second pillar

**Initial capital**:
The value assigned on 1 January 1999 to pension rights earned before the reform, for people born after 1948. Polish: *kapitał początkowy* (art. 173–175 FUS).
_Avoid_: starting capital

**Contribution base**:
The monthly income on which a person's pension contribution is calculated. Polish: *podstawa wymiaru składek* (art. 18 SUS).
_Avoid_: salary, wage, base

**Pension contribution**:
The 19.52% of the contribution base paid for old-age insurance, split between the account, sub-account and OFE. Polish: *składka na ubezpieczenie emerytalne* (art. 22 SUS).
_Avoid_: premium, tax

### The pension

**Pension capital**:
The sum, at retirement, of the valorized account, the valorized initial capital and the sub-account, which is divided by further life expectancy to give the pension. Polish: *podstawa obliczenia emerytury* (art. 25 FUS).
_Avoid_: calculation base, base, pension pot, savings

**Retirement age**:
The minimum age at which the old-age pension becomes available: 60 for women, 65 for men. Polish: *wiek emerytalny* (art. 24 FUS).
_Avoid_: pension age

**Further life expectancy**:
The average number of months a person of a given age is expected to live, from the GUS table, by which the capital is divided to give the pension. Polish: *średnie dalsze trwanie życia* (art. 26 FUS).
_Avoid_: divisor, annuity factor, G-factor

**Minimum pension**:
The amount to which a pension is topped up for people with enough qualifying years. Polish: *najniższa emerytura* (art. 85 ust. 2, art. 87 FUS).
_Avoid_: guaranteed pension, minimum benefit

### Growth over time

**Valorization**:
The yearly (and quarterly) growth of capital recorded before retirement (account, sub-account, initial capital) by an officially announced index. Polish: *waloryzacja składek* / *waloryzacja kapitału początkowego* (art. 25, 25a, 173 FUS; art. 40c, 40d SUS).
_Avoid_: valorisation, indexation, interest, return

**Indexation**:
The yearly growth, from 1 March, of a pension already in payment and its assessment base. Polish: *waloryzacja emerytur i rent* (art. 88–89 FUS).
_Avoid_: valorization, benefit valorization
